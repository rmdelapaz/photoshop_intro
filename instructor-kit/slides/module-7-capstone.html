Studio Session: Build It Well

A tidy file is a fast file.

The studio loop

Block in · refine · step back · finish

  • Big to small, never eyelash first
  • Name it the moment it matters
  • Group by role: Ctrl/⌘ + G
  • Color-label: right-click the eye icon
  • Snapshot before risky moves (History camera icon)
  • Save versions: capstone_v2.psd
  • Step back: fit · squint · View ▸ Flip Horizontal
Getting help today

Help flags on your monitor

  • Green: working, all good
  • Yellow: a question when you are free
  • Red: blocked for 5 minutes. Do not sit on it.

L22

Present & Ship

A piece, not a file. Done is a decision.

Critique norms

Honest, kind, specific

  • Critique the work, not the person
  • Point at the pixels; name a rubric row
  • Judge against the brief, not your taste
  • Presenter listens and says thank you, no defending
About 4 minutes each

The protocol

  • Present · 90 s: the piece in context + your two-sentence statement + a process shot
  • Squint test · 15 s: everyone squints; what do you see first?
  • Two reviewers · 90 s: "The first thing I notice is… what confused me was…" + one glow + one grow
  • Notes · 15 s: presenter writes the grow down
The rubric

The honest critique

  • Focal point: one thing wins first?
  • Light & color: consistent?
  • Edges & masks: halos at 100%?
  • Type: hierarchy, one or two fonts?
  • Believability: one light? AI artifacts?
  • Squint test: still reads?
GLOW what works, specifically GROW "I wonder if…"
