L08

Natural Skin: Heal, Don't Erase

Retouch the temporary; respect the permanent.

Would it be there next week?

Temporary vs permanent

  • No → heal it
  • spot · stray hair
  • shine · lint
  • Yes → keep it
  • freckles · moles · scars
  • laugh lines · nose shape
FIRST Survey at 100% and sort, before you touch a tool.
Clean skin

Heal on its own layer

  1. Save As portrait-working.psd · new empty layer retouch
  2. Spot Healing J: Content-Aware · Sample All Layers ✓ · click each spot
  3. Near edges: Healing Brush ⇧J · Sample: Current & Below · Alt/⌥-click clean skin
  4. Retouch layer opacity ~85–90% · toggle 👁 to check
WATCH Brush does nothing? Check the Sample setting for the tool you're using.

L09

Frequency Separation

Two sheets of skin: paint and texture.

The mental model

Split texture from color

  • Blur smooths both → plastic
  • Split them → fix each on its own terms
  • Color on LOW, texture on HIGH
The recipe · 8-bit

Separate

  1. Stamp Visible Ctrl+Alt+Shift+E / ⌘⌥⇧E · duplicate ×2 → LOW (lower), HIGH (upper)
  2. LOW: Gaussian Blur until blotches and pores just vanish (~6–12 px)
  3. HIGH: Image ▸ Apply Image · Layer LOW · Subtract · Scale 2 · Offset 128
  4. HIGH blend mode → Linear Light ✅ looks identical again
16-BIT Add · Scale 2 · Offset 0 · Invert. Wrong recipe = gray mess.
Then fix each sheet

Smooth

  • LOW: Lasso a blotch → Feather 10–20 px → small blur (or soft brush, low opacity)
  • HIGH: Healing Brush / Clone Stamp · Sample: Current Layer
  • Toggle HIGH off to see the pure color sheet
MANTRA Color on LOW, texture on HIGH: never mix them.
Lab · 30 min

Separate & smooth

  • Recipe card in view the whole time
  • Checkpoint: after Linear Light, identical to the original?
  • Even blotches on LOW · pores stay crisp
  • One texture fix on HIGH · Save
STUCK? Delete HIGH, duplicate the stamp again, redo Apply Image.
L10

Dodge & Burn: Sculpting Light

Paint light back onto the face.

The gray layer trick

50% gray · Soft Light

  1. Alt/⌥-click New Layer · Mode Soft Light · tick Fill with Soft-Light-neutral color (50% gray) · name D&B
  2. Soft brush · ~10% opacity · D then X → white = dodge
  3. X → black = burn
MANTRA Dodge the light, burn the shadow, 10% at a time.
Where it goes

Follow the existing light

  • Dodge: forehead center, nose bridge, cheekbone tops, chin, brow bones
  • Burn: sides of the face, temples, under cheekbones, under the jaw, eye sockets
WATCH Light from the left? Dodge left-facing planes, burn the right.
Finish & ship

Eyes, teeth, export

  1. Curves · brighten slightly · mask black · paint white on eye whites & teeth
  2. Clean, not glowing (optional: tiny Curves on the irises)
  3. Save the .psd · Export As JPG ~80% → portrait-final.jpg
Lab · 25 min

Sculpt & finish

  • D&B layer: dodge, then burn, at ~10%
