# Module 6 Labs: AI & Speed (Session 7)

Three labs, one per lesson. L17 and L18 are generative (they need internet, an Adobe
sign-in, and generative credits); L19 is fully offline. Learners work on **their own
photos** where possible and fall back to the synthetic practice files below.

The course's `images/` folder is Adobe Stock licensed to the author and is **not**
redistributable. Never copy it into a lab. All practice files here are synthetic and are
generated by script into `labs/module-6/practice/`.

```
practice/
  l17-lake-sign.jpg            L17 demo + lab: remove the sign, add a boat
  l17-three-distractions.jpg   L17 fast finishers: three removals, one at a time
  l18-tight-portrait.jpg       L18 demo + lab: uncrop and reframe
  l18-horizon-strip.jpg        L18 seam / horizon test
  l19-input/                   L19: the folder learners Batch
    l19-batch-01.jpg … l19-batch-08.jpg
  l19-watermark.png            L19: placed during recording
```

Learners also create an **empty** `l19-processed/` folder beside `l19-input/`. Do not
ship it pre-made with files in it.

## Practice file specs (for the generator script)

All JPEGs: RGB, quality 92, 72 ppi metadata unless stated. "Noise" means per-pixel
Gaussian noise (sigma given, same value on R, G, B) added after drawing and clipped
to 0–255, so the flat shapes read a little more like a photo and give generative
fill some texture to match. Coordinates are in pixels from the top-left.

### L17: Generative Fill

**`l17-lake-sign.jpg`**: 2400 × 1600, landscape. Embed an sRGB ICC profile.
- Sky: vertical gradient y 0–900, `#7fbce8` at top to `#cfe8f6` at the horizon.
- Sun: filled circle center (2000, 280), radius 130, `#fff4cf`, Gaussian-blurred radius 6.
- Shoreline: band y 880–950, `#6ea86a`, with a few darker `#4f8a4c` ellipses (bushes)
  along the left 40% only.
- Water: gradient y 950–1600, `#3f7fae` to `#2b6088`; add 40 thin horizontal
  lighter streaks (`#5a95c2`, 2 px tall, random x/length 60–240 px) for ripples.
- **The flaw to remove:** a signpost on the shore at the right third: brown post
  (`#5b4636`) rect x 1540–1570, y 640–940; sign board rect x 1440–1670, y 600–720,
  fill `#e8e2d0` with a 10 px `#c0392b` border, and three dark gray horizontal
  bars inside it to suggest unreadable text.
- **Space to add into:** open water x 300–1100, y 1000–1400 left empty (learners
  add "a small sailboat" there).
- Noise sigma 4.

**`l17-three-distractions.jpg`**: 2400 × 1600, landscape beach. sRGB profile.
- Sky gradient y 0–700 `#9cc9ee` → `#e6f2fb`; sea band y 700–900 `#3b86b5`;
  sand y 900–1600 gradient `#e9d7a8` → `#d6bf86`.
- **Three distractions**, each separate and well spaced:
  1. An orange traffic cone (triangle, `#f28c28`, base 150 px wide, 220 px tall,
     with two white 20 px stripes) standing on the sand, base at (500, 1300).
  2. A gray trash can (rect 180 × 260, `#7d8288`, darker lid rect 200 × 30
     `#5d6268`) at x 1700–1880, base at y 1250.
  3. A power line crossing the sky: a black 5 px polyline from (0, 180) sagging
     to (1200, 300) and back up to (2400, 160).
- Noise sigma 5.

### L18: Generative Expand & Reframe

**`l18-tight-portrait.jpg`**: 1200 × 1600, **portrait** (the "phone photo").
sRGB profile.
- Sky gradient y 0–880 `#86b7e3` → `#dcebf7`.
- Distant mountains: two overlapping filled polygons in `#6f86a3` and `#5d7290`,
  peaks around y 600–700, spanning the full width and **running off both edges**
  (so the expand has to continue them).
- **Horizon:** a perfectly straight lake edge at y = 880 across the full width.
- Lake y 880–1600, `#4a86b3` → `#2e5f86`.
- **Subject jammed against the right edge:** a simple standing figure: head circle
  center (1080, 760) radius 60 `#f0c8a0`; body rounded rect x 1010–1150,
  y 820–1260, `#c0392b`; legs two rects `#2c3e50` down to y 1520. The figure is
  cut off by the right edge (x 1150 is only 50 px from the edge). This is the
  "cropped too tight" flaw.
- Noise sigma 4.

**`l18-horizon-strip.jpg`**: 900 × 1200, portrait. sRGB profile.
- Sky y 0–600 `#f3b37a` → `#fbe3c4` (sunset); ground y 600–1200 `#6b8e4e` →
  `#4d6b36`.
- **Horizon:** dead-straight line at y = 600, full width.
- **A strong diagonal:** a wooden fence of 3 px-wide dark brown (`#4a3526`) rails
  from (0, 1100) rising to (900, 760), with vertical posts every 90 px. It must
  **exit both side edges** so learners can check it continues on the same angle
  after expanding.
- Noise sigma 3.
- Purpose: expand to 16:9 (or 1:1) and verify at 100% that neither the horizon
  nor the fence angle jumps at the seam.

### L19: Actions, Batch & Droplets

Folder `l19-input/` with eight JPEGs of **deliberately mixed sizes and
orientations**, so Fit Image vs Image Size and the Override option matter. Every
file has the same layout so outputs are easy to compare:
- Background: diagonal two-color linear gradient (colors per file below).
- A 24 px solid border in `#ffffff` around the whole frame (lets learners confirm
  Fit Image never crops).
- A large centered number ("01" … "08") in white, bold (DejaVuSans-Bold if
  available, else Pillow's default font at size), height about 30% of the short
  edge, with a 4 px dark outline.
- A filled circle of diameter 20% of the short edge in the lower-left quadrant

## Learners' own photos: what to bring

Ask learners (in the Session 6 wrap-up) to bring 5 to 10 of their own photos:
- **L17:** one photo with a clear distraction (sign, trash can, stray person,
  power line) **and** some open space (sky, water, a plain wall or tabletop).
- **L18:** one photo that feels cramped (subject near the edge) or is the wrong
  shape, ideally a vertical phone photo with a clear horizon.
- **L19:** any 6 to 12 photos in one folder; mixed orientations are better.

No photos of their own? Free-license sources such as **Pexels** or **Unsplash**
work, after checking the license on each image. Do not use photos of identifiable
people for generative edits without their permission.

## Running the labs

**L17: Remove & Add.** Save as `generative-working.psd`. Remove with an empty prompt,
add with a short concrete prompt, cycle variations, refine on the generative layer.
**L18: Uncrop & Reframe.** Save as `expand-working.psd`. Crop tool, Fill: Generative
Expand, drag outward, Generate; reframe to a ratio; check the seam at 100%.
**L19: Record & Batch.** Record "Web JPG 1200" in Set "Export Web", test on
`l19-batch-02.jpg`, Batch `l19-input/` to `l19-processed/` with Override Action
"Save As" Commands ticked, then Create Droplet.

**No credits or no network?** Follow the fallback in the facilitator guide: teach L19
first, pair learners on shared credits, or do the same jobs offline (Remove tool /
Content-Aware Fill; Crop outward with Fill: Content-Aware Fill).
