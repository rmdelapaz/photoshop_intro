# Module 5 Labs — Design & Type (Session 6)

Module 5 builds **one poster file across three lessons**: `poster-working.psd`
(18 × 24 in at 150 ppi = 2700 × 3600 px, dark background). Learners set the type with a
clear hierarchy (L14), add crisp vector shapes and a Pen-drawn graphic (L15), then polish
with layer styles, place a hero image and a Smart Object logo, and export (L16). The
exported poster completes the Practitioner tier.

```
labs/module-5/
├─ README.md                      this file
└─ practice/                      synthetic practice files (generated by script, see specs below)
   ├─ l14-poster-copy.txt         the poster text brief (two briefs to choose from)
   ├─ l14-flat-type-before.png    a "no hierarchy" poster to diagnose
   ├─ l15-pen-practice.png        Pen tracing sheet with suggested anchor points
   ├─ l15-logo-to-rebuild.png     a simple logo to rebuild with Path Operations
   ├─ l16-hero-stage.jpg          poster hero image: a concert stage with light beams
   └─ l16-sponsor-logo.png        transparent sponsor logo for Smart Object reuse
```

> **Image licensing.** Do **not** use or hand out anything from the course site's
> `images/` folder. Those photos are Adobe Stock licensed to the author and are not
> redistributable. Labs use only the synthetic files below or the learner's own images.

## Bring your own (optional)

- **Your Module 4 composite** (the exported JPG, or better, the layered .psd) makes an
  excellent poster hero image, and ties the two modules together.
- **Your own event**: a club meeting, a bake sale, a band night. Learners who write their
  own copy are more invested. It needs at least four lines with clearly different jobs
  (name, date and place, details, fine print).
- **A photo** for the hero image: something with calm space at the top or bottom for the
  title. Free-license sources such as **Pexels** or **Unsplash** are fine after checking
  the license on the photo's page.
- **Fonts**: use fonts already installed or activate them from Adobe Fonts (included with
  a Creative Cloud plan). Pick two: one characterful display font, one clean text font.

## Practice file specs (for the generator script)

All images are sRGB. JPG quality 90. Coordinates are in pixels from the top-left.

### L14 · Type & Hierarchy

**`l14-poster-copy.txt`** · UTF-8 plain text, exactly this content:

```
POSTER BRIEF A: Summer Music Festival (the lesson's example)

Line 1 (title):      SUMMER
Line 2 (title):      MUSIC FESTIVAL
Line 3 (key detail): July 18 · Riverside Park
Line 4 (extras):     Live bands · Food trucks · All ages
Line 5 (fine print): TICKETS AT THE DOOR
Badge / path text:   EST · 2026 · LIVE
Sponsor:             Riverside Radio (logo: l16-sponsor-logo.png)

Document: 18 x 24 in at 150 ppi, dark background. Save as poster-working.psd.
Rules: every line its own type layer; 1st line boldly biggest; two fonts maximum;
one accent color on the most important line or a small "tickets" badge.

POSTER BRIEF B: Community Night Market (alternative)

Line 1 (title):      NIGHT MARKET
Line 2 (key detail): Friday, August 8 · 6 to 11 PM
Line 3 (place):      Old Town Square
Line 4 (extras):     Street food · Local makers · Live DJ
Line 5 (fine print): FREE ENTRY · FAMILY FRIENDLY
Badge / path text:   EVERY SECOND FRIDAY

Decide the reading order yourself before you size anything.
```

**`l14-flat-type-before.png`** · 1800 × 2400 px
- Background: flat dark navy (`#14213d`).
- The five Brief A lines (SUMMER / MUSIC FESTIVAL / July 18 · Riverside Park / Live
  bands · Food trucks · All ages / TICKETS AT THE DOOR) in **one** plain sans-serif font
  (for example DejaVu Sans), **all the same size (64 px), same weight, same color**
  (white `#ffffff`), left-aligned at x = 90, stacked from y = 90 with tight line spacing
  (72 px), leaving the rest of the poster empty.
- **The flaw to diagnose:** no hierarchy (nothing leads, the eye wanders), cramped
  spacing, no accent, everything jammed in the top-left corner.
- Use: shown at the L14 frame ("what do you read first?"); learners rebuild the layout as
  live type in their own document. It is a raster reference, not an editable file.

### L15 · Shapes & the Pen

**`l15-pen-practice.png`** · 2400 × 1600 px
- White background, a light-gray 3 × 2 grid of cells (800 × 800 px each, 2 px `#d6dee9`
  divider lines).
- In each cell, a template shape drawn as a **4 px mid-gray (`#8a94a3`) outline**, about
  560 px across, with **red dots (radius 9 px, `#e03131`) at the suggested anchor
  points**, and a label above it in 28 px dark text:
  1. `LIGHTNING BOLT · 7 points · click only`: a zigzag bolt polygon.
  2. `MOUNTAIN RANGE · 7 points · click only`: an open polyline of three peaks
     (bottom-left, peak, valley, peak, valley, peak, bottom-right), closed along the bottom.
  3. `LEAF · 2 corners + 2 curves`: two arcs meeting in sharp points at tip and stem,
     plus a straight center vein line (vein not required for tracing).
  4. `HEART · 4 points`: a classic heart (two top lobes as curves, a sharp bottom point,
     a sharp top notch); dots at the notch, the bottom point, and the widest point of
     each lobe.
  5. `CLOUD · 5 bumps`: a cloud outline made of five circular arcs on a flat bottom;
     dots where the arcs meet.
  6. `SOUNDWAVE · smooth`: a sine-like wave of 3 periods, open path; dots at each peak
     and trough, and short light-blue (`#31a8ff`) handle lines drawn from 2 of the
     dots to hint at click-and-drag.
- Draw curves with enough polygon segments (or Bézier sampling) that the outlines look
  smooth at 100 %.

**`l15-logo-to-rebuild.png`** · 1200 × 1200 px
- White background. Centered logo built from three simple shapes, flat colors, no
  outlines:
  - a filled orange circle (`#f08a3c`), radius 300 px, center (600, 520);
  - a smaller circle, radius 230 px, center (690, 450), **subtracted** from it (so the
    orange reads as a crescent);
  - a dark navy (`#14213d`) rounded rectangle 640 × 90 px (corner radius 45 px) centered
    at (600, 880), overlapping nothing.
- Bottom-right inset (a 250 × 250 px box with its top-left at (935, 935), clear of the bar, with a 1 px gray border and the
  label `HOW IT'S BUILT`): the same three shapes at 25 % scale drawn as thin dashed
  outlines only, so learners can see the underlying circles and bar.
- Use: fast-finisher challenge, rebuilt with the Ellipse and Rectangle tools and Path
  Operations (Subtract Front Shape).

### L16 · Layer Styles & the Poster

**`l16-hero-stage.jpg`** · 2700 × 3600 px (the poster's own size)
- Background: vertical gradient from near-black purple (`#120a24`) at the top to deep
  violet (`#3a1a5e`) at 70 % height, darkening again to black at the bottom.
- **Light beams:** 4–5 long triangular beams fanning down from points along the top
  edge, each a semi-transparent polygon (white or pale cyan / magenta, 10–18 % opacity,
  Gaussian blurred ~30 px), crossing each other.
- **Stage glow:** a wide soft ellipse of warm magenta-orange light (radius ~900 × 250 px,
  blurred) centered at (1350, 2900).
- **Crowd silhouettes:** along the bottom 20 %, a row of 25–35 black figures (circle
  heads of radius 45–60 px on rounded-rectangle shoulders), varied heights, with 6–8
  raised arms (thin rotated rectangles), slightly overlapping, all pure black
  (`#000000`) so they read as foreground.
- Keep the **top 40 % relatively calm** (beams only) so the title has room.
- Add fine grain noise (±4 per channel) to avoid banding.

**`l16-sponsor-logo.png`** · 800 × 800 px, **RGBA** (transparent background)
- A white (`#ffffff`) circle ring (outer radius 300 px, ring width 28 px) centered at
  (400, 330), containing a simple three-period sine "radio wave" line (12 px thick, round
  caps) across its middle.
- Below it, the text `RIVERSIDE RADIO` in bold white sans-serif, 64 px, centered at
  y = 720, with letter spacing about +8 px.
- Anti-aliased edges; no background pixels. White so it shows on the dark poster; learners
  can recolor it with a Color Overlay style.

## Lab summaries

**L14 · Fix the Hierarchy (24 min).** New 18 × 24 in, 150 ppi, dark document →
`poster-working.psd`. Type tool (T): every line its own layer (commit with Ctrl/⌘+Enter
or Esc, click empty canvas for the next line). Assign a reading order and size boldly.
Character panel: tighten title leading, add tracking to small all-caps labels. Two fonts,
one accent. Type on a path around an Ellipse set to Path mode.
**Done when** a viewer names the reading order at a glance.

**L15 · Vector Elements (23 min).** Warm up on `l15-pen-practice.png` (5 min max). Then
in the poster: Rectangle in Shape mode, Fill none + Stroke (frame); Ellipse + Path
Operations (badge on one layer); a Pen shape in Shape mode (click for corners, drag for
curves; refine with Direct Selection); a Pen path in Path mode turned into a selection
and a layer mask; the 400 % scale test against a rasterized copy.
**Done when** all five checklist items are ticked.

**L16 · Finish the Poster (34 min).** Title: Gradient Overlay + subtle Drop Shadow.
Badge: Stroke + soft shadow, then Copy/Paste Layer Style. Place Embedded
`l16-sponsor-logo.png` and a hero image (`l16-hero-stage.jpg` or the Module 4
composite) as Smart Objects. Align with smart guides. File ▸ Export ▸ Export As ▸ JPG.
**Done when** the screen JPG is exported and the layered .psd is intact.

**If time / homework:** the print-ready version (printer's spec sheet → Image ▸ Canvas
Size for bleed → Edit ▸ Convert to Profile on a copy or a PDF/X preset → File ▸ Save a
Copy as Photoshop PDF); a square social version reusing the same styles and Smart
Objects; a different color mood; one deliberately over-styled copy for comparison;
saving a favorite title treatment as a Style; rebuilding `l15-logo-to-rebuild.png`.
