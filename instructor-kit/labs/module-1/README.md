# Module 1 Labs: Foundations (Session 2)

Three lessons, each with a guided build (with the instructor) and a "Now You" solo
variation. The module deliverable is the L04 build: a messy photo, cleaned up and exported.

```
practice/                        generated synthetic files (safe to redistribute)
  l02-washed-sky.jpg             L02 guided build: pale sky over dark hills, darken the sky only
  l02-spotlight-object.jpg       L02 solo: dull object on a busy mid-tone background, spotlight it
  l03-subject-busy-bg.jpg        L03 guided build: bold subject on a cluttered background, cut it out
  l03-calm-background.jpg        L03 guided build: soft studio backdrop to place the cutout on
  l03-fuzzy-subject.jpg          L03 solo: dandelion puffball with wispy fibers on green, Refine Edge
  l03-other-scene.jpg            L03 solo: a warm sunset scene to place the fuzzy cutout on
  l04-dusty-sky.jpg              L04 guided build: dust spots, a bird, a pole + wire by a roofline
  l04-photobomb.jpg              L04 solo: beach with a trash can and a figure, remove two ways
```

> **Never use the course site's `images/` folder for labs.** Those photos are Adobe Stock
> licensed to the author and are not redistributable. The files in `practice/` are
> generated by script and can be copied to every learner machine.

> **About AI selection on synthetic files.** Select Subject is trained on real photos, so on
> a flat, drawn scene it may pick an odd region. That's fine: the lesson's FAQ fix
> (Object Selection tool, draw a box around the subject) works reliably on these
> high-contrast shapes, and it's a useful thing to see. Learners' own photos give the
> "real" Select Subject experience.

## Practice file specs (for the generator script)

All files: sRGB JPEG, quality 90, 8-bit RGB, no EXIF. Coordinates in pixels from the
top-left. "Noise" = per-pixel Gaussian noise on all channels, clipped to 0–255. Use a
fixed random seed per file so regenerated files are identical.

### `l02-washed-sky.jpg`: pale sky over dark hills (L02 guided build)

- **Size:** 2400 × 1600.
- **Sky:** vertical gradient RGB (205, 220, 235) at the top → RGB (240, 243, 247) at the
  horizon: pale and washed out. Add 6–8 cloud blobs (overlapping ellipses 250–600 px wide,
  RGB (248, 249, 252), Gaussian blur radius 35) that are barely visible against the sky;
  darkening the sky should make them pop.
- **Land:** two overlapping rolling hill silhouettes filling the bottom, drawn as polygons
  from smooth sine curves: back hill ridge y ≈ 900 ± 70 (RGB (55, 85, 50)), front hill
  ridge y ≈ 1050 ± 90 (RGB (40, 70, 38)). The horizon is therefore **irregular**, not a
  straight line, so a big soft brush and a soft seam matter.
- **Tree:** a single tree on the front hill at x ≈ 1650: trunk 30 × 180, RGB (50, 40, 30);
  canopy of 3 overlapping circles (radius 80–110), RGB (35, 60, 35), the top of the canopy
  rising **above** the ridge into the sky (a stretch goal for careful masking).
- **Texture:** grass-like noise on the land only (sigma 6), sky noise sigma 2.
- **Correct result:** sky deeper blue with visible clouds; hills and tree unchanged; soft
  transition along the ridge.

### `l02-spotlight-object.jpg`: dull object on a busy background (L02 solo)

- **Size:** 2000 × 1500.
- **Background:** mid-gray-brown "wallpaper": fill RGB (120, 110, 100), then ~600 random
  rectangles and circles (10–80 px) in muted tones within ±25 of that color, then
  Gaussian blur radius 6 (busy but out of focus).
- **Table:** bottom 25% of the frame, RGB (90, 70, 55) with horizontal wood-grain streaks
  (thin lines, ±12 in value).
- **Subject:** a simple vase standing on the table, centered at x = 1000: body an ellipse
  420 × 520 with a 140 × 160 neck rectangle on top, RGB (175, 95, 60) (terracotta), with a
  soft vertical highlight stripe (lighter by 30, blurred radius 20) on the left third.
- **The flaw:** everything is equally lit and equally dull (overall contrast reduced:
  output = 40 + input × 0.7), so the subject doesn't stand out. Brightening only the vase
  and darkening only the background should make it pop.
- **Noise:** sigma 3.

### `l03-subject-busy-bg.jpg`: bold subject on a cluttered background (L03 guided build)

- **Size:** 2000 × 1500.
- **Background (busy):** "confetti" clutter: fill RGB (150, 150, 150), then ~900 random
  shapes (rectangles, circles, short thick lines; 15–120 px) in random saturated and
  unsaturated colors, fully covering the frame. No blur: it should fight for attention.
- **Subject:** a coffee mug, centered at (1000, 800), large (about 45% of the frame
  height): body a rounded rectangle 520 × 600 (corner radius 40), RGB (200, 40, 45)
  (strong red); a handle on the right drawn as a thick ring (outer radius 150, inner
  radius 90, 60 px thick) so there's a **hole** in the handle that shows background (a
  check for careful selection); a darker ellipse at the top rim, RGB (120, 20, 25); a soft
  white highlight stripe on the left side (alpha 35%, blur radius 15).
- **Edge detail:** blur only the subject's outline by 1.5 px (draw the mug on its own
  layer, blur its alpha slightly, composite) so the edge is photographic, not aliased.
- **Noise:** sigma 3 overall.
- **Correct result:** mug on a layer mask with the handle hole masked out, placed on
  `l03-calm-background.jpg` or a solid color; no gray halo.

### `l03-calm-background.jpg`: soft studio backdrop (L03 guided build)

- **Size:** 2000 × 1500 (same as the subject file so it drops in without resizing).
- **Content:** radial gradient centered slightly above middle (1000, 650): RGB
  (205, 215, 225) at the center → RGB (120, 135, 150) at the corners, plus a subtle darker
  "floor" band in the bottom 20% (RGB darker by 20, soft transition over 120 px).
- **Noise:** sigma 1.5 (prevents banding). No objects.

### `l03-fuzzy-subject.jpg`: wispy puffball (L03 solo, Refine Edge Brush)

- **Size:** 2000 × 1500.
- **Background:** saturated, slightly textured green: vertical gradient RGB (70, 130, 60)
  → RGB (40, 90, 40), blurred leaf blobs (~150 ellipses 40–160 px, greens ±20, blur radius
  12). The strong green is deliberate: it creates a color fringe that Decontaminate Colors
  removes.
- **Subject:** a dandelion seed head centered at (1000, 650): a dense core disk radius 60,
  RGB (200, 190, 160); **~700 radial fibers** from the core outward to radius 260–330,
  each a 1-px line in RGB (245, 245, 240) at alpha 50–85%, with a tiny 3–4 px "parachute"
  tuft (short fan of 4–6 lines) at each fiber's tip. Blur the fibers layer by 0.8 px.
  Stem: a 14-px-wide slightly curved line from (1000, 710) down to the bottom edge, RGB
  (120, 150, 90).
- **Noise:** sigma 2.
- **Correct result:** fibers survive as soft, semi-transparent strands after Refine Edge
  Brush; little or no green fringe with Decontaminate Colors on.

### `l03-other-scene.jpg`: a different scene to place the fuzzy cutout on (L03 solo)

- **Size:** 2000 × 1500.
- **Content:** a warm sunset: vertical gradient RGB (250, 170, 90) at the top → RGB
  (200, 90, 80) → RGB (80, 50, 90) at the horizon (y = 1050); dark silhouetted ground
  below, RGB (40, 30, 45), with a gently curved ridge. Sun disk radius 90 at (1400, 980),
  RGB (255, 220, 150), blur radius 20.
- **Purpose:** its warm light clearly **clashes** with the cool, evenly lit puffball, so
  learners notice the mismatch the lesson says Module 4 will fix. Noise sigma 2.

### `l04-dusty-sky.jpg`: spots, a bird, a pole by a roofline (L04 guided build)

- **Size:** 2400 × 1600.
- **Sky:** vertical gradient RGB (95, 145, 205) → RGB (185, 210, 235) at y = 1100, with
  4–5 soft cloud blobs (blur radius 30).
- **Building (bottom-left to center):** a flat-roofed building from x = 0 to x = 1500,
  roofline perfectly **straight and horizontal** at y = 1100, wall RGB (170, 150, 125);
  a 20-px darker parapet band along the roof (RGB (120, 105, 90)); a row of 8 evenly spaced
  windows (100 × 140, RGB (60, 70, 85)) at y = 1250–1390. The hard roofline and repeating
  windows are what the Clone Stamp is for. Ground/street below y = 1450, RGB (80, 80, 85).
- **Distractions:**
  1. **Dust spots:** 28 spots in the sky, placed randomly (not on the building), each a soft
     dark circle radius 4–12 px, RGB (60, 60, 70), alpha 35–70%, blur radius 1.5. Keep at
     least 3 of them small (radius 4–5) so they're only obvious at 100% zoom.
  2. **Bird:** a dark "V"/gull silhouette ~70 × 30 px, RGB (35, 35, 40), in open sky at
     about (700, 380). (Easy Remove-tool target on a plain background.)
  3. **Utility pole:** a vertical dark rectangle 26 × 900, RGB (55, 45, 40), at x = 1520
     (just right of the building's right edge), from y = 700 down to the ground; a
     crossbar 160 × 14 at y = 740; one **wire**: a 4-px line from the crossbar's left end
     sagging slightly (quadratic curve) to the top-left corner at (0, 520). The pole runs
     right beside the building's straight right wall and roofline corner, so healing tools
     will smear the hard edge and the Clone Stamp is needed there.
- **Noise:** sigma 3 overall (so repairs have texture to match).
- **Correct result:** no spots at 100%, bird, pole and wire gone, roofline and building
  corner still straight and crisp.

### `l04-photobomb.jpg`: beach with two distractions (L04 solo)

- **Size:** 2400 × 1600.
- **Scene:** sky gradient (top 40%) RGB (120, 170, 220) → RGB (200, 220, 235); sea band
  y = 640–900, RGB (40, 110, 150) → RGB (70, 140, 170) with thin light wave streaks;
  sand below y = 900, RGB (215, 195, 155) with strong fine noise (sigma 10) and a few
  darker ripple lines, so the sand has real texture to rebuild.
- **Subject (keep):** a colorful beach umbrella left of center: a half-disk canopy
  (radius 220) at (800, 900) with alternating red/white wedges, a pole 12 × 320 down into
  the sand, and a soft shadow ellipse on the sand.
- **Distractions (remove):**
  1. **Trash can** at about (1850, 1180): a gray rectangle 150 × 230, RGB (110, 115, 120),
     with a darker lid band (170 × 26) and a soft shadow to its right.
  2. **Photobomber figure** at the right edge, partly cropped by the frame: head circle
     radius 45 at (2320, 760), body a rounded rectangle 130 × 380 below it, RGB
     (60, 50, 70), standing on the sand with its base at y ≈ 1200 and overlapping the sea
     line (so it crosses two textures).
- **Noise:** sigma 3 overall (sand sigma 10 as above).
- **Correct result:** both removed with each method, compared at 100%; any repeat or smear
  cleaned with the Clone Stamp.

## Lab steps (summary; full steps in the workbook)

**L02: Rescue the Sky.** `l02-washed-sky.jpg` → Save As `sky-rescue-working.psd` → ◐ ▸
Brightness/Contrast, Brightness down → click mask thumbnail, D, X, fill black
(Alt/⌥ + Backspace/Delete or Edit ▸ Fill ▸ Black) → B, X for white, big soft brush, paint
the sky → soften the seam at ~30% opacity → Save → Export As JPG 80% `sky-rescue-final.jpg`.
*Solo:* `l02-spotlight-object.jpg`: brighten + mask to the vase; second darkening layer
masked to the background. *Going further:* Ctrl/⌘ + I on a mask.

**L03: Cut Out a Subject.** `l03-subject-busy-bg.jpg` → Save As `cutout-working.psd` →
Select ▸ Subject (or Object Selection box) → Quick Selection (W) to add, Alt/⌥ to
subtract (don't forget the handle hole) → Select and Mask: View Overlay, Refine Edge Brush,
Smooth/Feather, Shift Edge slightly negative, Decontaminate Colors if fringed → Output To:
Layer Mask → new layer beneath (Ctrl/⌘-click New Layer icon) with a calm fill or
`l03-calm-background.jpg` → Save. *Solo:* `l03-fuzzy-subject.jpg` onto
`l03-other-scene.jpg`; note the color clash. *Going further:* Select ▸ Inverse.

**L04: Clean & Export.** `l04-dusty-sky.jpg` → Save As `cleanup-working.psd` → new layer
`retouch` → Spot Healing Brush (J), Sample All Layers, 100% zoom, click each spot → Remove
tool on the bird and pole/wire (or Content-Aware Fill from the photo layer, Output To: New
Layer) → Clone Stamp (S), Sample: Current & Below, Alt/⌥-click, fix the roofline and
building edge → 100% pass → Save → Export As JPG 80% `cleanup-final.jpg`.
*Solo:* `l04-photobomb.jpg`: remove both distractions with the Remove tool and again with
Content-Aware Fill; compare; clean tell-tale signs with the Clone Stamp.

**Definition of done (Module 1 deliverable):** `cleanup-final.jpg` exported from a .psd
whose fixes all live on separate layers, with no spots, smears, or repeats at 100%.

## Learners' own photos: what to bring to Session 2

1. **A landscape with a pale, washed-out sky** (or any photo with a clear top-vs-bottom
   split, e.g. a view out a window). L02.
2. **A portrait or object photo** for the spotlight exercise. L02 solo.
3. **A photo with one clear subject:** a person, pet, or product. L03.
4. **A harder subject:** wispy hair, fur, or a leafy plant. L03 solo.
5. **A photo with dust, a blemish, or a distraction:** a sign, a trash can, a power line, a
   photobomber. L04 (ideally the same "messy photo" carried through the module).

Portraits of other people: get their permission first, and prefer family, friends, or
self-portraits.

No suitable photos? Learners can download free photos from sites such as **Pexels** or
**Unsplash**. Check the license on the site before using a photo (both currently allow free
use for practice like this, but terms can change), and credit the photographer if the work
will be shared publicly.

Do not hand out solutions before the lab.
