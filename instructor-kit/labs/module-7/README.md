# Module 7 Labs: Capstone (Session 8)

The capstone is one self-directed piece built mostly from **the learner's own photos
and assets**, planned (L20) and largely built (L21) as homework between Session 7 and
Session 8. Session 8 is for finishing, presenting, and critique. There are no
step-by-step solution files: every capstone is different. Use the reference notes in
the Module 7 answer key to judge plans, files, and presentations.

The course's `images/` folder is Adobe Stock licensed to the author and is **not**
redistributable. Never copy it into a lab. The files below are synthetic backups,
generated by script into `labs/module-7/practice/`, for learners who have no
suitable photos or who arrive behind and need a ready-made brief.

```
practice/
  l21-backup-old-photo.jpg     "Old photo restoration" brief
  l21-backup-product.jpg       "Product hero shot" brief: the object to cut out
  l21-backup-backdrop.jpg      "Product hero shot" brief: the surface/backdrop
  l21-backup-sky.jpg           "Surreal composite" / poster background
  l22-mockup-wall.jpg          L22: a plain wall scene for presenting in context
```

## Practice file specs (for the generator script)

All JPEGs: RGB, quality 92, sRGB ICC profile embedded, 72 ppi metadata unless
stated. "Noise" means per-pixel Gaussian noise (sigma given) added after drawing
and clipped to 0–255.

**`l21-backup-old-photo.jpg`**: 2400 × 1800, landscape, 300 ppi metadata. A faded,
damaged "vintage print" to restore (healing, cloning, tone, color: Modules 1–3).
- Base scene, drawn then toned: a house silhouette (rect body x 700–1500,
  y 800–1400, triangle roof to y 550), a door and two windows as darker rects,
  a tree (circle radius 260 at (1900, 800) on a trunk rect), ground below y 1400,
  sky gradient above.
- Convert to grayscale, then apply a sepia tone (map gray g to RGB
  (g·1.0, g·0.86, g·0.66), clipped) and **reduce contrast**: remap levels to
  the range 70–205 (the "faded" flaw).
- **Uneven fade:** multiply by a radial gradient that is 1.0 at center and
  0.82 in the top-right corner (a color/brightness cast to correct).
- **Scratches:** 12 thin (1–3 px) near-white (`#f4ecdc`) lines, mostly
  vertical with slight random angle, random length 300–1500 px, including two
  that cross the house's front wall.
- **Dust spots:** 150 small specks, random positions, radius 2–6 px, half
  dark (`#3a2e22`) and half light (`#f6eedf`).
- **A torn corner:** a filled polygon in paper white (`#f8f4ea`) covering the
  bottom-left corner, roughly (0, 1550)–(260, 1800)–(0, 1800), with a jagged
  edge (10–15 random vertices).
- A 40 px off-white (`#efe6d2`) border around the whole image, like a print
  margin. Noise sigma 7 (film grain).

**`l21-backup-product.jpg`**: 2400 × 2400, square. A simple product on a plain
background, to cut out cleanly (Module 4).
- Background: flat light gray `#d9dcdf` with a very subtle vertical gradient to
  `#c9cdd1` at the bottom.
- Product: a ceramic mug. Body: rounded rect x 800–1500, y 800–1700, corner
  radius 60, fill a horizontal gradient `#2f6db5` → `#5b95d8` → `#2f6db5`
  (cylindrical shading). Rim: ellipse x 800–1500, y 760–840, `#244f86`, with an
  inner ellipse `#1b3c66`. Handle: a thick ring (outer ellipse x 1440–1720,
  y 1000–1500, inner ellipse inset by 70 px, transparent hole showing the
  background). One soft white vertical highlight stripe (40 px wide, blurred
  radius 12) at x ≈ 950.
- A soft gray contact shadow ellipse under the mug (x 760–1540, y 1660–1760,
  `#9aa0a6`, blurred radius 25), which learners will replace with their own.
- Noise sigma 3.

**`l21-backup-backdrop.jpg`**: 3000 × 2000, landscape. A surface for the product
hero shot.
- Top 60%: a smooth wall gradient `#f3e9dc` → `#e2d2bd`.
- Bottom 40%: a wooden tabletop: base `#9b6a43` with 30 horizontal grain lines
  (1–3 px, color jittered between `#86573a` and `#b07d55`, slight sine waviness),
  and a slightly lighter front edge band (y 1960–2000).
- A warm light falloff: multiply by a horizontal gradient 1.0 on the left to
  0.85 on the right (so learners must match light direction: light from the left).
- Noise sigma 4.

**`l21-backup-sky.jpg`**: 3000 × 2000, landscape. A dramatic sky for composites
and poster backgrounds.
- Vertical gradient `#1e2a55` (top) → `#c65f6a` (60%) → `#f2b56b` (bottom).
- 25 soft clouds: clusters of 4–8 overlapping white/pale-pink ellipses (alpha
  90–160) placed mostly in the middle band, each cluster Gaussian-blurred radius
  18–30.
- Leave the top 25% relatively clean (space for a poster title).
- Noise sigma 3.

**`l22-mockup-wall.jpg`**: 3000 × 2000, landscape. A plain room scene for
presenting a finished piece in context (L22) when no mockup PSD is available.
- Wall: y 0–1650, gradient `#e9e4dc` → `#d8d1c5`, with a soft light pool (a
  large radial gradient, alpha 60, centered at (1500, 500)).
- Floor: y 1650–2000, `#8a6a4e` with a 6 px darker baseboard line
  (`#5e4a38`) at y 1650.
- **A picture frame hanging center:** outer frame rect x 1050–1950,
  y 260–1460, fill `#2b2b2b`; white mat rect inset 40 px; **opening** (where the
  art goes) x 1150–1850, y 360–1360 (700 × 1000, a 7:10 portrait opening) filled
  with flat mid-gray `#9a9a9a`. Put a soft drop shadow below-right of the frame
  (offset 18 px, blur 30, `#000000` at alpha 70).
- Noise sigma 2.
- Learners: File ▸ Place Embedded their export (it arrives as a Smart Object),
  scale it into the opening, and add a soft Drop Shadow layer style (Module 5)
  so the art sits in the frame. Landscape pieces can be shown in a second copy with the frame
  redrawn landscape; the generator may optionally output
  `l22-mockup-wall-landscape.jpg` (same scene, frame x 900–2100, y 330–1270,
  opening 1000 × 700).

## What learners should bring (their own photos)

Tell learners at the end of Session 7, by brief:
- **Poster**: a hero photo (subject with clear edges), a background, the text.
- **Surreal composite**: 2 to 4 photos shot in similar light (same direction,
  similar time of day), one with a clear subject.
- **Retouched portrait**: one well-lit portrait, ideally with permission to use
  it in class.
- **Old photo restoration**: a scan or phone photo of a real old print (flat,
  evenly lit, no glare).
- **Product hero shot**: a product photographed against a plain background, plus
  a surface or setting photo.
- **Brand mini-kit**: the brand name, 2 or 3 colors, and any photos for the
  social set.

No suitable photos? Free-license sources such as **Pexels** or **Unsplash** work,
after checking each image's license. Fonts should be ones the learner is licensed
to use (Adobe Fonts via their plan, or the system fonts). Mockup PSDs from design
sites are fine for class use only if their license allows it; otherwise use
`l22-mockup-wall.jpg`.

## Running Session 8

1. **Plan review (20 min):** pairs, one-line brief, moodboard, current file,
   "What do you expect this to look like?" Instructor triages red / yellow / green.
2. **Studio (90 min):** help flags, circulation rounds, timed cues at 0:55 (step
   back), 1:20 (stop adding), 1:40 (export `firstname-capstone.jpg` in sRGB to
   the shared folder), 1:45 (two-sentence statement).
3. **Break (12 min):** load files on the projector in presentation order.
4. **Presentations + critique (50 min):** 90 s present, 15 s squint test, two
   reviewers (one glow + one grow on a rubric row), 15 s notes. Pods of 4–5 for
   12+ learners.
5. **Close (8 min):** where to go next, the one idea to keep, final journal.

Generative steps (Generative Fill/Expand) need internet, an Adobe sign-in, and
credits; learners should finish them at home before Session 8.
