What You Get

For every module (×8)
  • Facilitator guide: timing, live-demo scripts, the beginner mistakes to pre-empt, checks for understanding
  • Participant workbook: objectives, worked examples, write-in exercises, self-check quizzes
  • Answer key: every quiz answered and explained, plus lab reference notes
  • Slide deck: a self-contained projector deck (no software to install)
  • Labs with practice files: generated, royalty-free images you can hand straight to learners
Across the kit
  • 8-session syllabus (3 hours each) in web and print formats: weekly, twice-weekly, or a 4-day intensive
  • Student setup guide, including what photos to bring to each session
  • Final capstone assessment: six brief options, a 9-row rubric built on the course's own critique questions, a peer-critique protocol, and a scoring sheet
  • The live course website, with 22 lessons and a 25-card Recipes library learners keep using after class
  • Print-ready PDFs throughout: hand out or project

Why Instructors Choose It

  • Minimal prep to teach. The facilitator guide scripts each lesson: timing, demos, and the exact beginner mistakes to head off. Walk in and deliver.
  • Beginners actually finish. A first win in Session 1, then a finished, exported piece every session, so learners never leave with half a project. The capstone gives them a portfolio piece to show.
  • The professional habits, from day one. Non-destructive editing, masks, and Smart Objects run through every module, and each core idea comes back at greater depth, so nothing has to be unlearned.
  • Current and responsible. Generative Fill and Expand are taught as tools in service of craft, with prompts, limits, ethics, and disclosure. Retouching is taught with consent and restraint.
  • No image licensing headaches. Labs use generated practice files and learners' own photos, so there's no stock to license for your class.
  • Flexible delivery. Weekly for eight weeks, twice a week, or a 4-day intensive. Community college, continuing ed, corporate L&D, library, or solo trainer.
