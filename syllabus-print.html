Photoshop: From First Win to Finished Work

A hands-on beginner course in Adobe Photoshop. Over eight sessions you fix, grade, retouch, composite, design, and automate, finishing one real piece every session, then plan, build, and present a portfolio piece of your own.

How the Course Runs

One module, one 3-hour session, one finished deliverable. The course is built as eight modules (0 to 7), and every module already ends in a finished piece: a fixed photo, a clean photo, a graded photo, a portrait, a composite, a poster, a batch, a portfolio piece. So the schedule follows the modules instead of cutting across them. Each module's lessons (framing, a live demo, a hands-on lab, and a quick self-check) add up to about 2¼ to 2½ hours, which fits a three-hour session with a break, a recap, and a gallery or critique of the finished pieces. Nobody goes home with half a project, and every session starts from a clean slate.

The two ends of the course use their time differently. Module 0 is a single short lesson, so Session 1 also covers orientation, a setup check, and a workspace tour, and runs the first win twice: once on a practice file, once on your own photo. Module 7 is a self-directed capstone: you choose your brief at the end of Session 7, plan and build most of it as homework, and Session 8 is for plan review, a final studio block, presentations, and peer critique.

Weekly: one session a week for eight weeks. The week between sessions is for practice on your own photos and your learning journal.
Twice a week: four weeks. Keep at least one day between Sessions 7 and 8 for the capstone build.
4-day intensive: two sessions a day (morning and afternoon). Hand out the capstone briefs at the end of Day 3 so the plan and most of the build happen before Day 4.

Foundations Sessions 1–3 (Modules 0–2) Practitioner Sessions 4–6 (Modules 3–5) Advanced Sessions 7–8 (Modules 6–7)

Course Details

Requirements

  • No prior Photoshop experience: absolute beginners welcome
  • Comfortable finding, copying, and organizing files and folders
  • A Windows or macOS computer that runs Adobe Photoshop 2025 or newer (the current release is recommended); the desktop app, not Photoshop on the web or iPad
  • An Adobe account with a plan that includes Photoshop (a free trial can cover a 4-day intensive, not an eight-week run)
  • Internet access and generative credits for Session 7 (Generative Fill and Expand run in the cloud)
  • A mouse (strongly recommended over a trackpad); a pen tablet is optional

What's Provided

  • A setup guide to complete before Session 1
  • The full 22-lesson course workbook (yours to keep)
  • Practice files for every lab, so you are never stuck without a suitable photo
  • The online Recipes library: 25 quick, standalone how-to cards
  • Guided builds in every session, a capstone studio, and peer critique
  • A finished, exported piece from every session, plus a presented portfolio piece

Bring Your Own Photos

  • S1: an everyday photo that is a little dark or crooked (a practice file is provided too)
  • S2: a landscape with a sky, a photo with one clear subject, and a "messy" photo with dust or a distraction
  • S3: a flat or off-color photo (overcast or indoor is ideal)
  • S4: a portrait of someone who has agreed to be retouched and shown in class (a self-portrait works)
  • S5: two images: a subject on a plain-ish background, and a scene to place it in
  • S6: optional: a photo for the poster's hero image (your Module 4 composite works well)
  • S7: a photo with a distraction and open space, a tightly cropped photo, and a folder of copies to batch
  • S8: your capstone file, built as homework from images you own or have the right to use

Assessment

  • Session deliverables: each module's finished piece, checked against its lesson's Project Completion Checklist
  • Learning journal: a short entry after every lesson, revisited at the capstone
  • Capstone: planned, built, presented, and scored on a published rubric: the six rows of Lesson 22's Honest Critique (focal point, light & color, edges & masks, type, believability, the squint test) plus plan, file, and presentation
  • Completion: every session deliverable submitted, plus a capstone with no rubric row below 2 of 3
  • Below the bar? Targeted feedback and a resubmission

What You'll Be Able to Do

Graduates leave with a portfolio of finished pieces and the habits to keep making more.

Work non-destructively with layers, masks, adjustment layers & Smart Objects
Make clean selections and believable cutouts
Grade light and color with Camera Raw and Curves
Retouch a portrait naturally with frequency separation and dodge & burn
Build a composite that looks like a single photograph
Design with type, shapes, and layer styles
Use Generative Fill & Expand, and automate with Actions and batches
Plan, build, and present a self-directed portfolio piece
