Ps From First Win to Finished Work

Learn Photoshop the way professionals actually work — one finished piece at a time. You'll make something real in your very first sitting, and every stage after that hands you a deliverable you'd be proud to show.

📚 What Makes This Course Different

Most software courses march you through menus: here's the Brush tool, here's the Clone tool, here's a dialog box you'll forget by lunch. This one doesn't. Every stage is built around a finished deliverable — a fixed photo, a cleaned-up image, a graded portrait, a poster — and you learn each skill at the exact moment the project needs it.

It's also spiral: the four ideas that make Photoshop make sense — non-destructive editing, the layer stack, masks as grayscale, and selections — come back again and again, each time a little deeper. You don't "finish" layers in week one and move on. You keep meeting them until they're second nature.

You will be able to…

  • Work non-destructively — make bold changes you can always dial back or undo
  • Think in a layer stack and control exactly what shows through with masks
  • Make clean selections and cut a subject out of its background convincingly
  • Grade light and color, retouch a portrait, and build a believable composite
  • Use Smart Objects, Camera Raw, Actions, and Generative Fill — the things that separate Photoshop from a basic photo app
  • Finish and present a portfolio piece you chose yourself

Who this is for

  • Complete beginners who've never opened Photoshop
  • Self-taught editors who can push sliders but don't feel in control
  • Anyone who's bounced off "tool tour" courses and wants to actually make things

🧠 A word before you start

If you've ever felt like Photoshop is "too much," that feeling is normal — and temporary. You are not going to learn all of Photoshop. Nobody has. You're going to learn the handful of ideas the whole program is built on, and then use them. Add the word yet to anything that feels hard today.

🗺️ The Learning Path

Eight stages, three tiers. Do them in order — each one leans on the last. Alongside them runs a parallel Recipes library you can dip into any time.

↔ Recipes Planned

Running alongside the path is a library of short, self-contained how-tos — the things you'll want to look up out of order:

  • "How do I remove a background?"
  • "How do I straighten a crooked horizon?"
  • "How do I whiten teeth without it looking fake?"
  • "How do I export for Instagram vs. print?"

Each recipe assumes nothing and links back to the stage where the idea is taught in depth. They're the reason you won't have to memorize everything up front.

✅ Prerequisites

Very little — this course starts from zero.

  • A computer that can run Photoshop comfortably (Windows or macOS)
  • Basic file skills: you can find a photo on your drive and know where your Downloads folder is
  • Curiosity and a photo you care about a little

Required software

  • Adobe Photoshop (2024 or newer recommended — Generative Fill in Stage 6 needs a recent version). A 7-day free trial is enough to get started.
  • That's it. No plugins, no extra downloads.

💡 About the pictures in this course

Because this is a self-contained web course, the panels and dialogs you'll see are hand-drawn diagrams and labeled mockups, not live screenshots of your Photoshop — so they stay crisp on any screen and in dark mode, and they highlight only what matters for the step. The before / after examples are real edited images you can wipe between. When a mockup stands in for the real interface, it's clearly marked so you always know what you're looking at.
